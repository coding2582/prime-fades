# Photo sources

Every image on this site is a real photo of Prime Fades (Everett or Marysville).
No stock photography is used. Sources are listed below so provenance is traceable.

## Read this before the site goes live

Being photos *of* the shop does not mean the shop *owns* them. Copyright normally
sits with whoever pressed the shutter. Before publishing:

- **Live in Everett photos** (the 5 best images here, including the hero) were shot by
  **Henry J** for the article *"Kicks and Cuts at Prime Fades"*. Ask Live in Everett for
  written permission. They profiled the shop favorably, so this is likely a yes, but get it in writing.
- **Yelp photos** were uploaded by Yelp users, not the shop. Permission would need to come
  from each uploader.
- **Google Business Profile photos** may have been posted by the business or by customers.
  Anything Jacob posted himself is fine to reuse.
- `work-marysville-fade.jpg` is a screenshot of an Instagram post by **@brookethebarber360**,
  captioned "Prime fades in Marysville wa". The Instagram UI chrome has been cropped off.
  Ask Brooke directly.

### The easy fix nobody has to negotiate for

Seven of the ten barbers keep Instagram portfolios of their own work (linked in the
Barbers section). Those photos were taken by staff, of cuts done in the shop. Getting
permission means asking your own crew, not chasing a newspaper or ten anonymous Yelp
accounts. Pulling a handful of the best cuts from those accounts would replace most of
the borrowed images here in an afternoon.

Beyond that, an hour with a decent phone across all three shops covers the interiors.
A credit line already sits in the site footer.

## Where the business details came from

- Services, durations, the 10 barber names, their Instagram handles, and Jacob's
  "shop owner / Marysville by appointment" note: the Square booking page
- Addresses, phone numbers, hours and email: primefades.net
- Rating and review count: Yelp listing for `prime-fades-everett`
- Shop history (MMA gym origin, 2016 move downtown, Gavin and the sneaker partnership):
  the Live in Everett article

Prices are deliberately not listed. The only figure found was "around $30" from the 2019
article, which is too old to publish. The site tells people to call or check at booking.
