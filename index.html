03 / The Shop

Cuts on California Street.

Jacob started cutting out of an MMA gym in Everett before he ever had a storefront. He moved into the California Street space in 2016 and has been downtown ever since.

It's a normal, good barbershop. Funko Pops on the shelves, hip-hop and sports on the TVs, white columns splitting the room, and a mural on the back wall that a local artist painted. Jacob and the crew have stayed open past close more than once to finish somebody's cut.
